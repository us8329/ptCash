Helping student PTs pay their way through clinical rotations

Physical therapy students work full-time, unpaid clinical rotations in new cities, with no housing support. PTcash connects them with local clients who need their help.

I'm a student PT I'm looking for help

The problem

40 hours, $0 pay

Clinical rotations are full-time work, but students earn nothing for them.

New city, no housing

Rotations send students to unfamiliar cities, and universities rarely help with rent.

On their own

With no time for a regular job, many students take on debt just to finish their degree.

How it works

Students sign up

Student PTs create a profile with their school, rotation city and availability.

Clients find them

Local clients browse students nearby and book sessions directly.

Students get paid

Students earn money during their rotation, and clients get affordable, motivated help.
